1. Water purification

Since water accounts for over 90% of the beverage, it undergoes strict multi-stage purification. Factories pass local water through sand beds, activated carbon towers, reverse osmosis systems, and ultraviolet chambers to eliminate sediment, chlorine, organic odors, and bacteria.

2.Syrup Blending

The localized base syrup is formed by dissolving sweeteners (such as refined cane sugar or high-fructose corn syrup) into the purified water. Technicians then mix this simple syrup inside giant stainless steel tanks with the highly guarded, natural lemon and lime essential oil concentrates supplied directly by Coca-Cola.

3. Proportional Mixing & Carbonation

Automated software precisely blends the flavored concentrate with additional purified water—typically at a standard ratio of five parts water to one part syrup. This liquid is chilled down to roughly 34°F (1°C) before carbon dioxide (CO₂) gas is injected under intense high pressure to give Sprite its signature crisp fizz

4. Packaging and High-Speed Bottling

While the liquid is being prepared, plastic PET preforms are blow-molded into their recognizable shapes. Bottles and cans are thoroughly sterilized before moving onto high-speed conveyor belts. A counter-pressure filling system fills the containers at speeds up to 1,200 bottles per minute to prevent foaming

5. Quality Control and Distribution

High-speed cameras instantly inspect fill levels. Bottles are slightly warmed to room temperature to prevent condensation, laser-coded with tracking metrics, labeled, and wrapped into multi-packs. Robotic arms stack the finished packs onto pallets, preparing them for global shipment

Recent Shift in Sprite Manufacturing

In a major global packaging overhaul, the Coca-Cola Company transitioned Sprite’s iconic packaging from its traditional green translucent plastic to clear PET plastic. While green plastic can be recycled into single-use items like carpets or clothing, it cannot easily be recycled back into new food-grade beverage bottles. By switching to clear plastic, Sprite factories have significantly enhanced circular recycling capabilities, allowing the old bottles to be repeatedly remanufactured back into new clean beverage bottles.
